How to Use a Mac mini Without Its Own Keyboard and Mouse

October 2026 · 5 min read

A Mac mini comes with no keyboard and no mouse. Plenty of people never buy any: the mini sits under a monitor next to a MacBook, and the MacBook's keyboard, or the one Magic Keyboard on the desk, does all the typing.

That setup has two problems. The first is getting through setup with nothing to type on. The second shows up every day: your Magic Keyboard, Trackpad, or Mouse can only pair with one Mac at a time. Once they're on the MacBook, the desktop has nothing left to click with. This guide covers both. The same advice applies to any desktop Mac you run without its own input devices.

Getting through setup with no keyboard

If you own Apple Magic accessories, you don't need anything else:

  1. Connect the Magic Keyboard to the Mac mini with its USB-C or Lightning charging cable.
  2. Wait a couple of seconds. macOS pairs any Magic device that's plugged in, automatically.
  3. Unplug it. The keyboard stays paired over Bluetooth.

Repeat with your Magic Trackpad or Magic Mouse. Each accessory you pair this way leaves the Mac it was on before. Pre-2015 models (the Apple Wireless Keyboard and the original AA-battery Magic Mouse and Magic Trackpad) have no port, so they need to be discoverable over Bluetooth instead: switched on and not connected to any other Mac.

No Magic accessories? Any wired USB keyboard and mouse will get you through setup. Newer Mac minis only have USB-C ports, so you may need an adapter.

Before you take the keyboard away: open System Settings > General > Sharing on the Mac mini and turn on Screen Sharing. If you ever end up stuck with no input, you can still reach the mini from another Mac.

Running it from your MacBook: 4 ways

1. Universal Control (free, same Apple Account)

With Universal Control, the MacBook's keyboard and trackpad work on the Mac mini's screen. Push the pointer past the edge of the MacBook's display and it appears on the mini's monitor, and whatever you type goes to the Mac under the pointer.

Both Macs have to be signed in to the same Apple Account, with Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and Handoff turned on, and the MacBook has to be open and nearby. The mini still needs its own display. Your devices stay paired to the MacBook, so once the MacBook leaves the desk, the mini has nothing again. More in our Universal Control comparison.

2. Screen Sharing (free, works across accounts)

Screen Sharing shows the Mac mini's whole screen in a window on your MacBook, and your keyboard and pointer control it. Enable it on the mini as described above, then connect from the MacBook: the mini appears in the Finder sidebar under Network, or you can open the Screen Sharing app.

It's the right tool for a truly headless mini, like a media server or build machine with no monitor attached. For everyday desktop work it's a step down: you're looking at a compressed video of a screen instead of the mini's own display.

3. Move the devices with a cable (free, slow)

The cable trick from setup also works every day. Plug the keyboard into the mini, wait, unplug, then repeat for the trackpad or mouse. Moving them back to the MacBook takes the same steps in reverse. It's fine once a week, but a chore twice a day. Our switching guide covers this and the manual Bluetooth method in detail.

Common questions

Can you set up a Mac mini without a keyboard and mouse?

Yes, if you have Apple Magic accessories. Plug a Magic Keyboard, Trackpad, or Mouse into the Mac mini with its charging cable and macOS pairs it automatically, then unplug it. Any USB keyboard and mouse also work for setup.

Can I use my MacBook's keyboard on a Mac mini?

Yes, through software. Universal Control lets the MacBook's keyboard and trackpad drive the Mac mini when both Macs use the same Apple Account, and Screen Sharing works across accounts. A MacBook can't act as a plain Bluetooth keyboard for another Mac.

Can I run a Mac mini headless?

Yes. Turn on Screen Sharing in System Settings > General > Sharing while you still have a keyboard connected. From then on you can control the Mac mini from another Mac on the same network, with no keyboard, mouse, or display attached.

How do I give my keyboard back to the Mac mini from my MacBook?

Either plug the keyboard into the Mac mini with a cable for a couple of seconds, or use a switching app that can send devices from the Mac you're using. Switchy 2.0 adds Send: pick a device in the MacBook's menu bar, choose the desktop Mac, and it re-pairs there.

Do I need a separate keyboard and mouse for my Mac mini?

No. One set of Magic accessories can serve a MacBook and a Mac mini. Share input with Universal Control, or move the pairing between the two Macs by cable or with a switching app.
